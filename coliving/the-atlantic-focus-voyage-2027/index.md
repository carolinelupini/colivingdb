## About

A transatlantic crossing on Virgin Voyages' Scarlet Lady from Miami (April 24, 2027) to Barcelona (May 8), calling at Las Palmas, Casablanca and Valencia. The cabin is the ticket: accommodation and the programme — five workshops, a BarCamp and a peer advisory lab, coworking labs, meetups and group dinners — both come with the fare, along with onboard wifi and Virgin's signature dining. Organised by William Campbell of Out of Office Travel, a Virgin Voyages travel agency; the programme is subject to Virgin's final onboard schedule.
