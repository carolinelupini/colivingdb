## About

Alicante Nomad Summit runs a full week rather than a weekend: talks, workshops, coworking sessions, sport, guided city walks and evening socials across several venues in Alicante, with the Cámara de Comercio on Calle Cervantes as the main conference space. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organiser negotiates preferential rates with several Alicante hotels and publishes the list. 2027 is the third edition, themed "Alchemy", and the second at week length after the 2025 debut was a two-day conference. The organisers expect 250+ attendees, 30+ speakers and 30+ countries. Sessions are in English.
