## About

TADAIMA's month-long nomad programme in Shimoda on the Izu Peninsula, 2.5 hours from Tokyo, built around four free public days on November 5–8. Accommodation not included — book separately; most one-month members stay at Kazemachi coliving (booked directly, coworking included there), or rentals from about ¥90,000/month. Membership adds reserved seats at the community dinners and events.
