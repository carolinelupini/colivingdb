## About

The first Arab Nomad Fest: seven days in Taghazout, on Morocco's Atlantic coast, run by the Arab Digital Nomads community, with a main conference, workshops and side events. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organisers share recommended areas once you register, and attendees get discounts through local partners. Meals, drinks, surf lessons and outdoor activities aren't in the ticket either, and the closing ceremony at the Timlaline dunes is an add-on at 250 MAD, arranged through the organisers' local operator.
