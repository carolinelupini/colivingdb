## About

A ten-day workation program in Fukuoka built around the two-day Ikigai Academy, where 30-plus speakers talk through how they rebuilt the way they live and work, plus the Nomad Impact Summit, Synapse Festival and daily meetups and workshops, run alongside RAMEN TECH, the city's startup conference. It opens on September 30 with the World Nomad Conference, free to attend and supported by Fukuoka City. The 2025 edition drew 496 participants from 57 nationalities. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organisers publish a neighbourhood guide with community rates at partner properties, and their Hoppin coliving program (listed separately on colivingDB) runs September 29 – October 13.
