## About

Ten days on Siargao, capped at 150 remote workers, entrepreneurs and digital nomads. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organisers send a list of places to stay in General Luna, from about $20 a night at the budget end to $100+ for beachfront boutiques. The conference venue, the coworking spaces and most of the places you'd stay are within walking distance of each other. Mornings are keynotes and workshops, afternoons are unconference sessions and activities, with a wellness day in every ticket and a closing beach party on October 25. Flights, meals, scooter rental and insurance are on you too. Run by BayBayIn Hub, which has held the event on Siargao since 2024.
