## About

Pangea's four-week coworking chapter in Bansko, in Bulgaria's Pirin mountains. It starts as Bansko Nomad Fest 2027 (June 20–27) ends. Accommodation not included — book separately (Pangea helps you find a place). The pass covers the community side: events and dinners, a local host and orientation, and a WhatsApp group. A coworking space isn't included either; Pangea points you to the best spots. Confirmed to run. Registration is on Luma, and the host approves each application. Disclosure: Caroline Lupini, who runs colivingDB, is part of the Pangea team.
