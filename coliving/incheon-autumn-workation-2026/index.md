## About

Two weeks in Songdo, the planned smart district on Incheon's waterfront, from Digital Nomads Incheon with Digital Nomads Korea and Hoppin, backed by Incheon City: founder exchanges, IFEZ ecosystem tours and policy sessions during the week, and island weekends at Muuido with a pop-up workspace by the sea. The one-day Incheon Nomad Fest runs inside it. English is the working language. Only the Full Pass includes a room; with the Event Pass, book your own.
