## About

Nomadbase's fourth Tarifa gathering, a week of talks, workshops, excursions and evening socials at the southern tip of Spain. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organisers pass on recommendations and deals for nearby places, plus an online space to team up with other participants on shared apartments. Sessions sit mostly outside working hours, so it runs as a workation rather than a week off. 150 people came in 2026; the 2027 edition is built for 300, and the full programme lands closer to the event.
