## About

Three weeks across two Setouchi cities. October 11–25 is Hiroshima: workshops, skill-sharing in Japanese and English, community dinners and cultural experiences with local residents. On October 25 the group takes the ferry across the Seto Inland Sea for a week in Matsuyama, a hot-spring and castle town on Shikoku. Accommodation not included — book separately, for both legs if you do the full run. Meals, transport, coworking and the ferry fare are on you too. Passes are sold per city, so one leg on its own works. The theme is "Yohaku" (余白), Japanese for blank space or margin.
