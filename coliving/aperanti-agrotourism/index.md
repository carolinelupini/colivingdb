## About

A seasonal coliving in a restored 1915 stone house in the village of Pera Orinis, at the foot of the Machairas Mountains about half an hour from Nicosia. Four private rooms with ensuite bathrooms, a courtyard, and a coliving manager; the hosts have run the agrotourism for over 12 years and ran their first coliving season in 2025.
