## About

A converted fisherman's pier building in Henningsvær, the self-styled cultural capital of Lofoten, built on the town's cod-fishing heritage. Ten guests across three private and two shared bedrooms, all with double beds and waterfront views — closer to a house than a hotel. SEASONAL: open 15 September 2026 to 25 February 2027 only, a fall/winter property for northern lights, polar twilight and powder. There is no midnight sun here; the property is shut through summer. On the work setup, in their own words: there is no dedicated coworking room and no external monitors — you work from the lounges. Wi-Fi speed is not published anywhere.
