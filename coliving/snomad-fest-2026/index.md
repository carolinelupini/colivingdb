## About

The winter edition, third time round: seven days splitting mornings between the slopes and a coworking room, with keynotes, unconferences, a storytelling night and a pitching competition filling the rest. The ski pass inside the ticket covers 4 Vallées/Verbier plus the Grand St Bernard. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organiser points attendees to Swiss Alps Coliving. The ticket also expects about two hours of group meal prep a day; breakfast and dinner are covered, lunch is not.
