## About

Nomad Week calls itself one part conference, one part festival: a week in Cape Town run by Work Wanderers and Pangea, and 2027 is the third edition. The Conference Ticket covers the welcome mixer, the opening ceremony and speed networking, a conference day of keynotes, panels and the Africa Pitch Competition, a coworking week with daily workshops, wellness activities, evening socials, the villa pool party and a closing rooftop party. Accommodation not included — book separately; the yacht brunch, the Winelands day out and the Winelands weekend away are add-ons booked separately. The ticketed programme runs February 17 to 26, and the organiser's own description gives the festival as 17 to 28 February. The venue is still to be announced.
