## About

A seven-day founders' week on Madeira's south coast, run by The Nomad Escape, which has held a December event on the island since 2019 — it ran through 2025 as Nomad Island Fest. Around 100 people, with talks, workshops and connection sessions. Only the Premium pass includes a bed; Basic ticket-holders book their own accommodation. Premium guests stay in one of two 4-star oceanfront hotels, a few minutes' walk from each other and from the auditorium. Cancel 60 or more days out for a 95% refund; inside 60 days you can transfer your place or roll it to the following year.
