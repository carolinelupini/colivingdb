## About

A two-day business summit on the Santo Domingo Malecon, built for founders, investors, policymakers and operators working between the Caribbean, Latin America and the US. The agenda runs keynotes and flagship panels on day one into executive roundtables, a pitch lab, university and workforce spotlights and a cultural excursion on day two, with a VIP dinner on the Malecon between them. Accommodation not included — book separately; the ticket covers the conference only, and the venue is the Crowne Plaza. Originally scheduled for August 2026, the summit was rescheduled to April 8–9, 2027, and the organiser says existing General Admission and VIP registrations carry over.
