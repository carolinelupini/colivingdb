## About

Running since 2015, for founders, freelancers, investors and operators. Ten days in all: a welcome evening on January 15, two conference days (January 16–17) at the Shangri-La Hotel Chiang Mai, a 48-hour Buildathon that pairs attendees with local Thai developers and designers, then Nomad Week (January 18–24), seven days of side events any ticket holder can host — 56 of them across 20+ venues in 2026. Accommodation not included — book separately; many attendees stay at the Shangri-La, and Chiang Mai has guesthouses and boutique hotels within reach of the venue.
