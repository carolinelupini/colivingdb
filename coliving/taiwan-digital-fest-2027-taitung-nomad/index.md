## About

Four weeks, one per zone, with 270-plus programs across 60-plus venues: Nanhui in the south (Indigenous craft and the welcome market), the inland Valley (rice terraces, red oolong and hot-air balloons), the Coast (surf, a beach music festival and ocean tours), then Taitung City (talks, open-source sessions and a closing sunset party). Accommodation not included — book separately; the festival negotiates rates at a home-base hotel in Taitung City, and the cohort stays in local guesthouses during the regional weeks. Each of Taiwan Digital Fest's three legs is sold on its own.
