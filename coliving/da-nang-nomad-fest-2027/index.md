## About

Ten days in Da Nang from the Nomad365 team, who also run Athens Nomad Fest: two days of keynotes and panels at Furama Resort, an AI day, LinkedIn and short-video bootcamps, a hackathon with Swinburne University students, coworking days, day trips to Ba Na Hills and Hoi An, a pickleball tournament, and opening and closing parties. Accommodation not included — book separately; the organisers' free city guidebook covers where to stay. It's aimed at online freelancers and solopreneurs: coaches, consultants and content creators.
