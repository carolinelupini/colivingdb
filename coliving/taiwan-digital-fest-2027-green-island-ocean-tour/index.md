## About

A fifteen-seat coda to the Taiwan Digital Fest month, on a volcanic islet 33 km off the Taitung coast: sunrise at Niutou Hill, snorkelling at Chaikou Reef, a coastal cycling loop and a soak at Zhaori's seabed hot spring. Surf lessons, dives, a glass-bottom submarine and stargazing are optional extras. Accommodation not included — book separately; the association holds a block of rooms in Nanliao village guesthouses at a group rate. A scooter needs a valid international licence.
