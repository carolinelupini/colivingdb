## About

A small coworking and coliving house in central Kyoto — one building, three private rooms upstairs, a shared kitchen and lounge, a workspace with monitors and a deep bathtub, four residents at most. About ten minutes' walk from Shijo and Gojo stations; no lift. Run by Masaya Koshimoto, who founded Kyoto's digital nomad meetup.
