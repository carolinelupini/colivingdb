## About

Bansko Nomad Fest takes over a Bulgarian mountain town for a full week each June, running Sunday to Sunday. The week mixes a curated conference track, community-led unconference sessions that any attendee can propose and host, a morning wellness program, and an evening lineup in the town park that runs from speed dating and karaoke through to the bonfire night and the pool party at Pulse Terme. The 2025 edition drew 800+ attendees and 50+ speakers. Accommodation not included — book separately; the festival's Where to Stay page points at five colivings in town, apartment rentals from about €300 a month, and hotels from around €170 for the festival week. Meals are not included either, and the organisers do not provide Wi-Fi at the venues, though the town square and cafés have free access. Sofia Airport is the closest gateway, about two and a half hours out by bus or transfer. Tickets are online only, non-refundable, and convert to app credit rather than cash if you cannot attend.
