## About

A week-long festival that puts Palermo itself in the programme: morning talks, panels and keynotes at Villa Niscemi, a villa on the edge of Parco della Favorita, then afternoons and evenings in the city with local hosts. The organisers keep it to around 100 participants, roughly half locals and half nomads. Accommodation not included — book separately; the festival negotiates discounts with Palermo hotels and hostels, and a shuttle runs from Politeama up to Villa Niscemi, so staying in the historic centre works. The 2026 edition ran March 8–15.
