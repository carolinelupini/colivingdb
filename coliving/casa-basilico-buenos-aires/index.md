## About

Casa Basilico's month-long chapter in a 600 m² villa in Colegiales, on the Palermo side of Buenos Aires: eight bedrooms, a pool, a garden with an asado grill, and two community managers living in. Shared dinners, cooking classes and weekend trips are organised; food and weekend activities cost extra. For a coworking day out, Palermo's spaces are about 20 minutes away.
